Fourteen Owners Walked Into a Room. Only One Universe Walked Out.

The inaugural auction of the League of Friendship wasn't just a draft — it was a creation myth. Fourteen friends gathered at the Commissioner's home, auction dollars in hand, and bid the multiverse into existence. Hasan opened the night at $221 for Josh Allen and set the price of everything. Kimbo assembled a stack so quietly nobody noticed until it was too late. And the Commissioner? He blew half his budget on two players because they were his guys. This is the story of how it all began.
